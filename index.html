FAQ

Helpful answers before you reach out.

A few common questions about timing, setup, and customization.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and peak spring or fall dates, booking early is always recommended. Smaller events may have more flexibility depending on availability.

Do you deliver and set up the flower wall?

Yes — delivery and setup are available based on your event package and location.

Can the flower wall be used outdoors?

Outdoor use may be possible depending on weather, surface conditions, and overall event setup. Covered spaces are typically the best option.

Do you offer custom styling?

Yes — add-ons such as signage, balloon styling, and coordinating decor accents can help personalize the final look.
