About Williamson Flowerwalls

A local floral backdrop brand created for beautiful celebrations.

Williamson Flowerwalls is built around one simple idea: every meaningful event deserves a backdrop that makes the room feel special. Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before someone reaches out.

A good FAQ reduces repetitive messages and helps clients feel more ready to book.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and peak spring or fall dates, booking early is best. Smaller events may have more flexibility depending on availability.

Do you deliver and set up the flower wall?

Yes. Delivery and setup can be included based on your package and location.

Can the flower wall be used outdoors?

Outdoor use may be possible depending on weather, ground conditions, and event setup. Covered spaces are usually the safest option.

Do you offer custom styling?

Yes. Add-ons like signage, balloons, and coordinating decor accents can help personalize the installation.
